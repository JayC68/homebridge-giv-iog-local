GivHome setup

GivHome

Local GivEnergy battery control, Intelligent Octopus Go automation and Apple Home visibility — automatically, locally and clearly.

Welcome

Set up local battery control for Apple Home, Intelligent Octopus Go and optional Excess Energy Export.

1

Find your Battery Serial Number

Open the GivEnergy app while your iPhone is on the same WiFi network as your inverter or battery.

Settings → Local Monitoring → Scan for your inverter
  • Tap scan.
  • Copy the Serial Number shown for your inverter.
Paste the Serial Number here before moving on.
2

Enter your solar display setting

Your solar system size helps the Apple Home solar tile scale cleanly. Use the approximate maximum output of your solar PV system.

This is used for display scaling only. It does not control your inverter.
3

Connect Intelligent Octopus Go

Open Octopus Energy in a browser, not the app, and log into your account.

  • Copy your Octopus Account Number and paste it below.
  • Go to Personal Details → Developer Settings → API Access.
  • Scroll to Your API key, copy it, then paste it below.
Not using Intelligent Octopus Go? Leave these Octopus boxes empty and use your own off-peak times in the next step.
4

Confirm charging defaults

Most Intelligent Octopus Go users can leave these settings as they are.

Most users should leave this at 100.
5

Optional: Excess Energy Export

After setup, you can enable Excess Energy Export from the normal GivHome plugin config if you want GivHome to sell surplus battery energy later in the evening while keeping a configurable reserve before cheap charging resumes.

Plugins → GivHome → Plugin Config → Excess Energy Export
Leave this disabled unless you want automated evening export behaviour. It is opt-in and uses local GivTCP controls only.
6

Save settings

Press the Save settings button below to complete setup.

After saving: GivHome will save your settings into Homebridge. If Homebridge asks to restart, allow it to restart so your Apple Home tiles update.
7

Occasional maintenance

GivHome is designed to run quietly in the background. Occasionally, Homebridge may show updates for the operating system, Node.js or GivHome itself.

Applying updates every few months is recommended and usually takes only a few minutes. GivHome also shows an Update Available tile in Apple Home when a newer plugin release is detected on npm.

Local-first: battery control commands stay on your home network. Octopus details are only used to read Intelligent Octopus Go smart charging slots.
