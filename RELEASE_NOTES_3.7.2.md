# GivHome v3.7.2

v3.7.2 is a quality and reliability release focused on telemetry safety, history accuracy and schedule verification.

## Highlights

- Improved stale telemetry handling.
- Automation now safely pauses when live inverter data is out of date.
- Eve Energy history no longer records misleading stale telemetry.
- Improved inverter write verification and diagnostics.
- Improved Evening Excess Export schedule cleanup.
- Better resilience during temporary GivTCP communication interruptions.

No configuration changes are required.
